# Proposal, approval, and reservation policy — design draft

Research Agent → versioned Strategy → nonexecuting TradeProposal → deterministic Risk Engine → separate human approval → Broker Adapter. This is an intended future architecture, **not** an enabled order path. Risk passage is not human approval; human approval cannot override a failed or missing risk check. A proposal binds exact account, symbol, side, whole-share quantity, limit, fees, session, expiry, policy digest, portfolio snapshot digest, quote evidence, thesis, and strategy version. Mutation creates a new proposal ID and evaluation. An approval binds that digest, approver identity, time, and one-use idempotency key; proposed expiry is five minutes and also ends at the earliest quote/state/session invalidation. Price or portfolio changes trigger full re-evaluation even within five minutes. Rejection, expiry, or policy change invalidates approval.

Only a separately authorized human role may approve or reset a stop; an agent or LLM cannot grant execution rights. Proposal and approval credentials remain separate. At future submission, recheck policy, kill switch, environment, fresh quote/state, cash, reservations, loss stops, approval digest and expiry atomically. Audit creation, evaluation, approval/rejection, every state transition, and broker receipt. Duplicate approval and submission keys must be rejected idempotently. Existing synthetic workflow behavior does not qualify a live broker path.

## Durable reservation design for v1.1

Use a PostgreSQL transaction keyed by account and experimental sleeve. Lock the reconciled cash/sleeve inventory row and active reservations; calculate settled cash, buying power, attributed cost, open broker commitments, and pending proposal commitments in one serializable decision. Persist a unique proposal/idempotency key, digest, amount (limit notional plus fees/buffer), state, expiry, and event sequence before exposing an approval-ready result. Two concurrent proposals cannot claim the same unreserved cash. Same-symbol conflicts require a deterministic reject or explicit serialized replacement, not independent acceptance.

On partial fill, transfer filled cost to position inventory and keep only unfilled commitment reserved. On broker-confirmed terminal cancel/reject/expiry, release the verified remainder exactly once. Requested cancellation, disconnect, unknown status, or process restart retains reservation until broker status and ledger reconcile. Expiry of an unsubmitted proposal can release under an audited transaction; submitted-order expiry cannot. Reconciliation conflict freezes new entries and reservations, triggers escalation, and never treats missing orders as freed cash. Recover persisted state on restart before processing proposals. No production reservation service is implemented in v1.0.
