**Design only; no candidate was implemented or backtested.** The authoritative baseline is the immutable [v0.6 manifest](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v06/manifest.json), result digest `30f79857dfd68eca48af178cb6cf04f446988c6c3d20e3945fddd6d66c4f7311`. The 2025 normalized $100,000 result is +1.03% return, +18.20% VOO adjusted-all exploratory proxy, −17.18 percentage points excess, 8.43% drawdown, 127 sides, 63 round trips, 12.54× turnover, 55 re-entries, 5.42× re-entry buy turnover, 29.02% average exposure, and $765.14 modeled commissions plus fill impact. This is retrospective evidence, not qualifying validation. No disagreement was found between those rounded figures and the v0.6 machine-readable summary.

The [hypothesis registry](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v07/hypothesis-registry.json) fixes four questions before future work. **H01** tests a fresh-event re-entry reset after exits. **H02** separately tests making the existing 20-session holding boundary a review rather than a mandatory exit. **H03** is diagnostic only: decompose low exposure into technical eligibility, signal generation, hypothetical allocation, cash, position constraints, fill timing, and exits. **H04** tests the unchanged baseline under three fixed transaction-cost scenarios. The two candidate designs are independent; no combined candidate is preregistered. A reduced turnover or higher exposure does not itself establish better returns or risk-adjusted performance.

The [future protocol](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v07/experimental-protocol.json) fixes the 2023–24 development and 2025 validation windows, selected nine-symbol swing universe, VOO current-vintage adjusted-all proxy, $100,000 normalized capital, next-session-close whole-share fills, $0.10 commission, 2-bp full spread, 5-bp slippage, no borrowing, and no separate cash-dividend credit. H01 and H02 each receive one change against the locked v1.0.0 baseline; candidate configuration digests must be recorded **before implementation or any run**. All inputs and results must be immutable and replay exactly. The 2026 OOS is sealed and cannot be opened to resolve uncertainty.

**The 2025 period is already research-informed.** Its v0.6 results have been inspected, so later candidate comparisons on it are exploratory and potentially selection-biased, never a fresh independent holdout. Any effect estimate must show sample counts, period variability, adverse outcomes, data-quality gaps, and the complete candidate set. No automatic winner selection or promotion to `QUALIFYING_HISTORICAL` or `FORWARD_PAPER` is permitted. The [comparison metric registry](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v07/comparison-metrics.json) fixes behavior, performance, and implementation outputs in advance. A single retrospective return improvement cannot pass a candidate.

H04 fixes three scenarios for unchanged v1.0.0: matched no-cost counterfactual (0 commission/spread/slippage), baseline ($0.10, 2/5 bps), and stressed ($0.25, 4/10 bps). A future matched no-cost replay would measure a simulated return difference; the existing $765.14 is modeled gross friction only and **not** that difference. Cost effects may change fills and trade frequency, which must be reported. Candidate comparisons use baseline costs, not whichever cost scenario favors a candidate.
