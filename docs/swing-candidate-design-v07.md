## `SWING_REENTRY_RESET_CANDIDATE` (H01)

**Change:** On a *completed fill* exiting any held symbol—trend failure, ATR stop, or holding limit—disarm new entries for that symbol. Observe only complete, available sessions after the exit. Once a full session fails the original entry predicate, arm the symbol. A **later** complete session satisfying the original predicate is the fresh entry event and may queue the ordinary next-session fill. A signal observed while disarmed never becomes a retroactive order. There is **no minimum waiting period** beyond the two distinct post-exit observations; no duration is selected from returns. If a required bar, adjustment vintage, RSI, volume, or ATR is missing/uncertain, do not treat it as a proven predicate failure or new entry event; remain disarmed and record missing data. The rule reads no future session at decision time.

State transitions: `HELD → EXIT_FILLED_DISARMED → OBSERVED_PREDICATE_FALSE_ARMED → OBSERVED_PREDICATE_TRUE_PENDING_ENTRY → HELD` after a valid fill. Existing-position restrictions and exit priority remain v1.0.0. Potential mechanism: fewer repeated buys after a recently completed sale. Adverse effects: missed continuations, lower exposure, worse return, or unchanged turnover if failures are frequent. Test only against the locked baseline with identical data/cost/capital settings. Evidence for the mechanism requires the number and timing of re-entries to change as specified, with exact replay and no missing-data/look-ahead bypass. Human review must weigh returns, drawdown, exposure, costs, and per-symbol harms; no automatic acceptance on lower turnover. Reject implementation if state transitions cannot be audited, missing data can arm an entry, or the candidate requires an unapproved policy change. Historical action/vintage and survivorship limits remain.

## `SWING_HOLDING_EXIT_CANDIDATE` (H02)

**Change:** At the existing 20-session holding boundary, log `HOLDING_LIMIT_REVIEW` but do **not** sell solely for elapsed time. Keep the unchanged ATR stop and trend-failure exits active and higher priority on every eligible session. Once either triggers, use the ordinary next-session counterfactual exit. The v1.0.0 immediate re-entry behavior remains unchanged for this candidate; H01 is not combined. Missing required exit data must produce an explicit review/missing-data event, never an inference that a stop or trend is safe. Any extended holding duration and interaction with policy limits require human approval before implementation.

State transitions: `HELD → HOLDING_LIMIT_REVIEW_HELD → EXIT_PENDING_ON_ATR_OR_TREND → EXIT_FILLED`; without a verified exit condition the position remains flagged for review, and no new entry is created. Potential mechanism: fewer mechanical holding-limit exits and repurchases. Adverse effects: larger losses, longer underwater holdings, less cash available, higher concentration, or policy conflict. A valid future comparison must isolate this one rule, show holding-time tails and unresolved review states, and report all return/drawdown/turnover/exposure trade-offs. Reject implementation if ATR/trend exits can be suppressed, missing exit evidence is silently treated as safe, a policy maximum is breached, or a human has not approved the removal of the mandatory exit. The candidate has no assumed profitability advantage.

H03 exposure work is diagnostic only. Instrument each session's complete-bar technical eligibility, candidate proposals, hypothetical sizing and affordability, pending/filled orders, existing positions, exits, available cash, simultaneous-position limits, and concentration. Label Risk Engine outcomes `NOT_EXERCISED` unless an explicitly separate risk-review path is designed; v0.6 had **no observed Risk Engine rejections**. Attribute exposure gaps to signal scarcity, allocation constraints, or simulator timing without describing counterfactual fills as broker activity.
