# Phase v0.8 — human review

**Exploratory results only. No strategy winner is selected.** The [historical evidence review](historical-evidence-review-008.md) and immutable [final experiment registry](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v08/final/registry.json) contain the evidence and digests.

| Question | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Did H01 follow its specified state rule? | Yes. After each completed exit it disarmed the symbol; the exit session did not arm it; a later complete false observation armed it and a distinct later true observation could queue a next-session buy. The full audit records 57 exit disarms, 57 false-arm events and 49 later-true events in 2025. Missing data could not arm it. |
| Re-entries and turnover? | Re-entries **55 → 49**; turnover **12.54× → 11.35×**; sides **127 → 115**. |
| Missed continuations? | 11 otherwise eligible 2025 signals were suppressed while disarmed. They are opportunities foregone under the baseline predicate, not proven profitable trades. |
| Return, drawdown, exposure, costs? | Return **+1.03% → +0.45%** and average exposure **29.02% → 27.61%** worsened; drawdown **8.43% → 7.00%** and modeled friction **$765.14 → $692.69** decreased. Development return and drawdown both worsened. |
| Why low baseline exposure? | Original entries were technically eligible on 52 of 249 decision sessions, at most three symbols per session; 1,444 nonheld symbol-sessions lacked a signal. Existing positions and pending fills also constrain deployment; categories overlap. Fixed 10% target size matters. No historical Risk Engine acceptance/rejection was observed. |
| Fixed H04 scenarios? | 2025 no-cost **+1.7877%**, baseline **+1.0262%**, stressed **+0.2712%**; all had 127 sides. The matched no-cost minus baseline simulated return difference is **+0.7615 pp**, distinct from $765.14 direct modeled friction. |
| $1,000 feasibility? | 2025 whole-share skips **230 at $160 → 142 at $1,000**; filled symbols **3 → 8**; maximum positions **1 → 3**. Maximum single-name concentration remained **98.83%** at $1,000, while drawdown was **10.99%** versus 25.50% at $160. These are different feasible portfolio paths, not proof of strategy merit. |
