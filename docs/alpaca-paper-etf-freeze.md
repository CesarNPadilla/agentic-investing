The owner's exact ETF-only instruction remains in [the scope decision](examples/alpaca-paper-month/etf-scope-decision.json). SCHB is an engineering selection for controls testing within that scope, not a separate claimed human approval or a research strategy. [Schwab's issuer page](https://www.schwabassetmanagement.com/products/schb) identifies it as an ETF; the authenticated, read-only [Alpaca asset check](examples/alpaca-paper-month/etf-asset-read.json) returned active and tradable on October 6, 2026. Broker asset class `us_equity` alone was not used to classify it as an ETF.

The new [frozen specification](../conf/paper-etf-plumbing.json) and `PAPER_ETF_CONTROL_PLUMBING` version 1.0.0 produce SCHB intents only. The historical INTC strategy and its durable ownership namespace remain unchanged and disabled for this trial. ETF order IDs use a distinct namespace; historical INTC claims cannot be adopted as ETF ownership. A dedicated ETF durable-claim/ownership integration is still required.

Sizing chooses the smallest whole-share quantity meeting the unchanged $25 minimum, with cent-rounded buy limit checked against cash, the original sleeve limits, current-account 10% concentration and the $100 fixture ceiling. The fixture ceiling is an additional trial constraint, not a change to executable capital policy. One entry per NYSE session and a later reconciled exit of owned shares are retained. Existing foreign shares are never liquidated. Every intent still requires the full risk review; sizing is not admission.

The actual pre-open IEX quote request succeeded at HTTP level but returned a zero ask. The strict reader rejected it. No valid quote, current price fit, initial tradable status or runtime qualification is claimed. Repeat during a regular session; do not substitute an indicative price or relax freshness/positivity rules.

The ETF intent contract rejects INTC, preserves whole-share limit/day/non-extended orders and inherits the existing prohibition on real broker mutations. Tests cover identifier isolation, rounding at the exposure boundary, invalid quotes, cash/exposure limits, foreign holdings, owned exits and mock-only submission. They do not establish a real broker round trip.

Next integration work is ETF-specific durable claims and reservation/restart fixtures, then ETF quote/status/event routing. Existing INTC read-only diagnostic paths are historical components, not an ETF runner. Account-flow publication, status bootstrap/continuity, externally authenticated reset, persistent process/event supervision and final launch qualification remain blockers. The historical 23/36 (64%) checklist remains an archived component snapshot; it is not a newly qualified ETF readiness percentage. No paper orders, live orders or trial-clock startup occurred.
