# ADR-026: isolated risk diagnostics around the authorized policy

Status: implemented for synthetic v0.9 research diagnostics only.

Use the existing pure Investment Policy evaluator as the sole source of current policy checks. Wrap it with explicit quote/session/proposal-ID inputs and deterministic audit fields. Keep the $1,000 what-if annotation separate from the $160 policy snapshot. A passing diagnostic still requires human review and has no execution capability. Do not connect the wrapper to broker order methods. Durable conflicting-order reservations, trusted clock/data, and undefined policy maxima remain future work.
