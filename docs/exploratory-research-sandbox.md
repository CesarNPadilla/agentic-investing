# Exploratory retrospective research sandbox v0.6

`ExploratoryResearchRunner` is an isolated, read-only counterfactual runner over the fixed 2023-01-03–2024-12-31 development and 2025-01-02–2025-12-31 validation periods. It refuses altered periods, any dataset session/bar/fundamental, raw bar, or corporate action whose economic date crosses 2025-12-31, and inputs marked holdout-exposed. The 2026 OOS interval appears only as sealed plan metadata. Neither OOS signals nor OOS benchmark returns were requested or calculated.

The runner pins the unchanged v1.0.0 strategy-definition digests and the baseline swing SMA50/SMA200, RSI14 50–75, volume20, ATR14 ×2 stop, and 20-session holding limit. Research-only `ResearchRules` remain separate from Investment Policy v0.2 hard controls. It records retrospective entries, exits, same-symbol re-entries, indicator states, whole-share skips, capital exposure, and costs. Signals and fills are counterfactual records, not TradeProposals or broker orders. Risk Engine outcomes are `NOT_EXERCISED_COUNTERFACTUAL_ONLY`; zero bypasses in this result are not a claim that the Risk Engine approved trades.

The source is a newly bounded Alpaca IEX `adjustment=all` snapshot for ten selected symbols and VOO, 2022-06-10–2025-12-31, 893 sessions per symbol and 9,823 bars. The snapshot was acquired after the historical period and is **current-vintage**, including possible later adjustments. It does not prove historical availability, complete actions, or survivorship neutrality. The raw IEX snapshot and v0.5 normalized action ledger are used only for separate VOO arithmetic context; no event may become a historical signal without a proved knowledge boundary. The cash-dividend estimate explicitly assumes event terms and coverage solely to perform arithmetic. It is not benchmark qualification. The actual comparison in the swing result uses VOO adjusted-all current-vintage total-return **proxy** for both periods; the price-only and nonreinvested cash-dividend estimates are separately labeled.

Simulated fills use next supplied session close, $0.10 commission per side, 5 bps slippage plus a 2 bps full spread (6 bps half-spread/slippage impact per side), no borrowing, whole shares, and no separately credited cash dividends because adjusted-all prices proxy the return basis. Direct execution friction is the sum of modeled fill-versus-adjusted-close price differences and commissions. This is an estimated gross accounting component, **not** a causal estimate of return recovered by removing costs; no cost-free alternate replay was run. The normalized $100,000 simulation measures strategy behavior. The distinct $160 whole-share, no-fraction simulation measures capital feasibility and is labeled `CAPITAL_FEASIBILITY_EXPLORATORY` in its report. Neither alters the policy sleeve or authorizes fractional orders.

The immutable full result and manifest are under the user's local `AppData/Local/AgenticInvesting/exploratory-retrospective-v06/runs/exploratory-retrospective-v06-swing-v1-locked/`. A compact machine-readable [manifest](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v06/manifest.json) and [summary](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v06/summary.json) are versioned in this repository. The manifest pins source-file hashes, dataset digest, source revision, definition digests, rule digest, periods, execution assumptions, and full-result digest. Identical inputs reproduce the canonical result digest exactly; changing assumptions requires a new experiment ID and immutable output directory. Earlier exploratory trial outputs were not overwritten.

This environment never invokes qualifying `historical_gates`, never starts paper validation, and cannot promote an exploratory class. Independent historical qualification remains blocked by corporate-action coverage/vintage and long-term source gaps. Human approval remains mandatory in any future trade workflow. Paper and production execution are disabled, and forward paper remains 0/60.
