# ADR-024 — controlled exploratory swing design

**Status:** Proposed for human review, 2026-10-01. **Scope:** v0.7 design and preregistration only.

## Context

The immutable swing v1.0.0 exploratory replay found repeated re-entry, holding-limit turnover, low average exposure, and material modeled friction. Its 2025 period has already been inspected; it is research-informed and cannot be reused as a fresh independent holdout. Corporate-action/adjustment qualification remains blocked. The human also proposed $1,000 experimental capital, while the current Investment Policy still caps attributed experimental capital at $160.

## Decision proposal

Keep the [v0.6 result](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v06/manifest.json) as the immutable comparison baseline. Register H01 re-entry reset and H02 holding-exit review as separate one-change candidates before implementation; keep H03 exposure decomposition diagnostic and H04 fixed cost sensitivity on unchanged baseline. No candidate receives a released strategy version or automatic winner status. Future reuse of 2025 is `EXPLORATORY_RETROSPECTIVE`, explicitly research-informed and selection-biased. Candidate modifications require recorded configuration digests before any run. A separate proposed-$1,000 capital-feasibility replay cannot establish strategy merit, change Investment Policy, or imply a deposit. Human approval is required for candidate specifications and any formal capital-policy change.

The 2026 OOS stays sealed. Existing qualifying gates, Investment Policy, Risk Engine, broker separation, and v1.0.0 strategies remain unchanged. Both strategies are NOT_ELIGIBLE; forward paper 0/60; paper and production execution disabled. See the [preregistration](strategy-experiment-preregistration-v07.md), [draft candidates](swing-candidate-design-v07.md), and [human review](human-review-007.md).

## Consequences

The next phase may implement only a separately approved, reproducible exploratory protocol. It cannot call 2025 a fresh validation set, use 2026 to select a candidate, infer return improvement from lower turnover, or route proposals to a broker. Adverse outcomes and uncertainty must be reported alongside any behavioral change.
