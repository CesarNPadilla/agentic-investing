# ADR-014: Investment Policy v0.2 and thesis-led research

Status: Accepted and implemented as nonexecuting interfaces, 2026-09-29.
Supersedes v0.1 policy defaults; preserves ADR-013 portfolio/capital separation.

## Decision

**Research -> Thesis -> Strategy -> Proposal -> Risk -> Human Approval -> Broker**

Never **News/LLM -> Broker**. This implementation stops at a persisted risk review.
Production execution and automatic rebalancing remain disabled. Existing holdings outside
purchase permissions remain valid to own, research and maintain theses for.

Adopt the [approved v0.2 policy](investment-policy-v0.2.md). Core 30-45%, Strategic 40-55%
and Satellite 10-20% are monitoring ranges. Concentration >20%/>30% yields informational
warnings, never liquidation. The $160 cap applies only to reviewed experimental capital.
Research initially includes holdings plus Mag7; purchase permissions remain separately
configured. No missing Mag7 member is automatically purchased.

Persist owner-supplied classifications and revisioned theses with UNKNOWN historical
rationale, immutable supplied original theses and append-only agent/material-event history.
News creates review requests; separate long-term/swing interfaces create proposals only.
Independent deterministic risk checks bind policy/proposal/thesis evidence, preserve required
human review, and fail closed on unknown controls or unreliable reconciliation. Swing
controls and validation evidence are separate and cannot relax parent limits.

## Consequences and limits

Timestamped analytics expose unknown classifications, strategy attribution and broker gaps.
Reliable references are required for sectors/industries/correlations; return history needs
adjusted, flow-aware, calendar-aligned evidence. Broker cash activity does not constitute a
complete atomic share/settlement ledger. Performance/validation source verification remains
an importer responsibility. No strategy signals, live news provider, autonomous thesis
rewrites, paper execution or production order integration are added.

Policy/risk schemas advance to v4; state remains v2. Observation/evaluation v4 tables are
additive. Previous evidence is not silently reinterpreted. Operational migrations and
PostgreSQL concurrency validation remain outstanding. See the
[implementation contract](investment-policy-portfolio.md) for endpoints, tests and decisions.
