# ADR-036: derive synthetic sleeve losses from frozen baselines and signed external flows

Status: v1.1f synthetic accounting foundation implemented.

Compute daily/cumulative net-equity P&L and positive flow-adjusted equity denominators from explicit initial/daily baselines and signed posted external flows. Preserve original N06/N07 thresholds and resets; deposits and day changes cannot clear a latch or revise cumulative funding baseline.

Persist accounting and latch atomically, reject silent baseline/flow history revisions, and use attached accounting for subsequent risk assessments. Missing/stale/nonpositive accounting blocks. Boundary/fee/ledger verification is fixture-only; trusted reconciliation, calendar-driven baselines, authenticated corrections and human reset remain unimplemented. See [owner review](human-review-011f.md).
