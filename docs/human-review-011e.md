# Phase v1.1e — synthetic evidence and digest-bound review foundation

**FOUNDATION COMPLETE / FULL v1.1 INCOMPLETE.** The next independent approved workstream adds quote, snapshot, session and review-binding qualification with synthetic fixtures. See [current implementation status](examples/risk-policy-v11e/implementation-status.json).

Strict envelopes record explicit fixture quote/snapshot/calendar source names, calendar version, policy/snapshot digests, currency/symbol, quote status/bid/ask, event and receipt timestamps, coherent snapshot cutoff/completeness/reconciliation, session schedule and clock/session verification flags. No real provider or operational owner was selected. Missing/invalid/naive/nonfinite inputs reject. Quote event age permits 30 seconds, snapshot age permits 60 seconds; future times or receipt-before-event block. Crossed/missing bid/ask, halted/unknown status and symbol mismatch block.

Regular-session fixtures use America/New_York open at 09:30 and a same-day supplied close no later than 16:00, allowing supplied early closes. A closed holiday fixture has no open/close timestamps; outside-session evaluation blocks, including the exact close boundary. The fixture is not a qualified holiday/DST database; calendar/source/clock flags are assertions for tests only. No extended-hours eligibility is added.

A synthetic review binds the proposal digest and the full evidence-envelope digest to risk-evaluation time. Missing review, changed policy/snapshot/quote/calendar/source inputs, future evaluation time or expiry at five minutes reject. Freshness/session gates can invalidate a binding earlier than five minutes. Review bindings carry no verified human identity/signature and grant no human approval or execution permission.

The evidence-aware PostgreSQL assessment path performs these checks inside the risk/latch/reservation transaction and persists the envelope/binding with diagnostic evidence. Symbol, quote time, snapshot cutoff, evaluation time and conservative price must agree with risk inputs; conservative buy price cannot be below envelope ask. Failed evidence prevents a new reservation. The original low-level synthetic diagnostic path is retained and does not require an envelope; it remains nonexecuting. Real clock refresh during retries and real provider trust remain unimplemented.
