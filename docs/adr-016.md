# ADR-016: Evidence-grounded long-term research, isolated from execution

Status: Accepted for v0.1 implementation, 2026-09-29.

## Decision

Add `agents.long_term` alongside the existing `ResearchOrchestrator`. Inputs are read-only
provider contracts, a trusted stored portfolio observation ID, and an optional reviewed
ResearchPlan. No broker, strategy, proposal, Risk Engine execution, LLM, shell, or tool
capability is injected. Research stops at assessments and thesis reviews.

Research Data -> Research Agent -> ResearchAssessment -> Thesis Engine -> Portfolio Intelligence.
Existing Portfolio Intelligence also supplies context to the assessment. Future strategies
must independently follow Proposal -> Risk -> Human Approval -> Broker; this service has
no such transition.

Research direction (`STRONGER`, `UNCHANGED`, `WEAKER`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INVALIDATED`,
`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`) is separate from the existing position-thesis lifecycle. Direction
compares outcomes of versioned, reviewed metric assumptions. A first/new-plan baseline is
UNDER_REVIEW; missing/ambiguous/incompatible inputs are insufficient; an evidenced explicit
invalidation may invalidate. Mixed improvement/deterioration or recovery from invalidation
requires review. News can require review, never supply a direction or modify a plan.
Cost basis is not an input to assumption evaluation. STRONGER is not BUY; WEAKER is not SELL.

Use additive versioned tables. An optimistic assessment-head update, thesis revisions,
completed-event receipts and completed-run audit commit together. Conflicts roll back all
research effects. The STARTED audit is durable before provider calls; failed runs retain
sanitized error codes. No hidden chain-of-thought is recorded. Existing thesis original
rationale, human notes and old revisions remain intact. Current thesis text may come only
from the explicitly reviewed plan; missing owner rationale stays unknown.

GOOG/GOOGL aliasing deduplicates issuer research only. Actual share classes, account holdings
and thesis records remain distinct. Universe comes from InvestmentPolicy plus stored actual
holdings, independently of purchase permission. Aliases and comparison symbols are configurable.

## Consequences

No new live provider or paid dependency is required. Unavailable data remains unknown.
Source reliability is an adapter attestation, not verification performed by the agent.
Valuation retains reported methods/units/bases/periods without universal thresholds, price
targets or blended ratios. News keyword relevance can produce false positives, explicitly
requiring research. No LLM interprets publisher instructions.

Research is an internal backend service, not an HTTP exposure or installed worker. A future
API must authenticate and authorize scope and observation ownership. A process crash leaves
a visible STARTED audit; automatic recovery, distributed leases, full migrations and
PostgreSQL concurrency validation remain operational follow-up work.
