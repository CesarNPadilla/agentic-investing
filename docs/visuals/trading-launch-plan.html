What closes the remaining gates?

Additional requirements before live money

Historical eligibility and 60 qualifying strategy sessions; acceptable performance/risk evidence; a live-ready broker and data/status feed (including the deferred Nasdaq requirement unless explicitly amended); verified production funding/account; deployed controls, reconciliation, key/recovery protection and kill switch; explicit human production and live permission. The current plumbing test supplies none of these investment-performance approvals.

Forecast assumptions and limits

Sandbox target assumes useful Webull answers by October 19–26 and roughly 2–4 weeks of remaining integration/qualification, partly in parallel, without a major redesign. Provider response timing and remaining effort are uncertain. Live scenario assumes a strategy becomes eligible before January, completes every scheduled session and passes its criteria. Missing evidence or extraordinary exchange closures moves dates later. The calendar estimates count approved early-close sessions as one session only if their evidence qualifies.
