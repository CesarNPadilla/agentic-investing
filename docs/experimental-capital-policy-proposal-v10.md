# Experimental sleeve proposal v1.0

**Current authorized:** $160 cap. **Proposed, unapproved:** $1,000 initial funded and attributed sleeve. No deposit, transfer, or real proposal is assumed. The existing investment portfolio is separate. $100,000 normalized research capital remains a statistical simulation convention; historical $160 results remain immutable.

For a future approved sleeve, require an owner-reviewed baseline identifying account(s), cash contribution, positions, cost basis, and any pre-existing orders. Available new-buy cash is the lesser of reconciled settled cash and broker buying power, minus all open-buy reservations and in-flight proposal reservations in the account. Sleeve capacity is the $1,000 approved allocation minus attributed acquisition cost and sleeve order/proposal commitments. Neither uninvested whole-account cash nor nonattributed holdings silently expands that capacity.

The proposed position ceiling is min($250, 25% initial funded sleeve, 25% current positive sleeve equity, unreserved settled cash); same-name marked holdings and commitments count together. Separate 25%-of-sleeve-equity same-name exposure and 75%-of-sleeve-equity aggregate exposure caps apply. For example, a single $500 whole share fails despite $1,000 total cash. If only one $200 holding exists, it is 100% of *deployed* exposure but 20% of sleeve equity: report both denominators, and use sleeve equity for the hard cap. The v0.8 observed 98.83% deployed-gross concentration was a warning, not an approved threshold. A separate diversification/exception rule remains a human decision.

Whole-share rounding is down to the largest quantity satisfying every cap, fees, and cash **only if** a new proposal is generated and re-reviewed; the Risk Engine itself does not silently resize a signed proposal. Below $25 proposed gross notional, reject. Partial fills consume reservation at actual cost; the unfilled remainder stays reserved until terminal broker-confirmed cancellation/rejection or reconciliation. A requested cancellation alone never frees cash. Competing proposals for a symbol or account must serialize under an atomic reservation; stale or conflicting state blocks.

Deposits do not reset cumulative P&L loss, do not increase the $1,000 cap, and do not count as profit. Any changed allocation or baseline requires explicit human-approved versioning. Existing holdings outside AAPL/MSFT/SPY remain valid holdings for analysis, but are not new-buy eligible.
