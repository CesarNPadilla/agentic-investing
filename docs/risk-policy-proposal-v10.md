## Decision hierarchy

Platform safety controls > environment isolation > approved Investment Policy > approved experimental-sleeve policy > strategy constraints > individual proposal risk evaluation > human approval. Lower layers may tighten but never relax an upper hard stop. Conflicting versions, absent required values, ambiguous account attribution, stale state, or missing evidence block new entries. Human approval does not override a risk rejection. A policy change needs an identified human approver, versioned signed/digested artifact, effective time, impact review, tests, and a separate deployment authorization. Any current proposals must be invalidated and re-evaluated against the new policy digest.
