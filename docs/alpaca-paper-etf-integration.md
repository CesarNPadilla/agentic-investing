# ETF ownership and diagnostic routing

The SCHB 1.0.0 intent model now has a separate durable `paper_etf_plumbing_claims` namespace and a mock-only integrated lifecycle harness. Tests exercise persisted preparation, unchanged identifiers/prices after restart, receipt/fill ownership agreement, buy cash consumption, sell share reservations, unsettled sale proceeds, same-day reentry prevention and next-day preparation. Historical INTC claims are never adopted. Foreign-symbol claims are rejected. Historical strategy files remain unchanged.

Current account-observation, event-monitor and status-probe command entry points load the strict ETF specification and route to SCHB. A changed symbol list, strategy version or activation flag blocks configuration. Historical unit-level INTC diagnostic defaults remain for legacy fixtures; they cannot enable broker mutations. The ETF event observer rejects an INTC event and latches before dispatching another risk check. Status subscription acknowledgement and transport pongs still cannot establish an initial ACTIVE state.

The [actual runtime audit](examples/alpaca-paper-month/etf-runtime-audit.json) records an active, unblocked, empty dedicated paper account. SCHB status authentication, subscription and three transport probes succeeded during a five-second observation; zero status events left status UNKNOWN. Today's exact New York midnight baseline was retained from Alpaca continuous portfolio history, with external-flow qualification still false. Alpaca's [documented status channel](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.1/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data) describes status events; this review does not infer a complete initial snapshot from a quiet subscription.

The startup risk observation encountered the previously documented zero-ask quote and failed closed at `QUOTE_READ_FAILED`. Its durable event journal entry remains unfinished and `PAPER_EVENT_OBSERVER_FAILED` is latched. Restart refuses incomplete journals. Nothing was deleted, marked successful, reset or released. This is a service recovery requirement, not proof of a daily-loss breach.

The owner selected Windows Hello/passkey in a separate local owner-control app. The [enrollment prototype](alpaca-paper-owner-passkey.md) is prepared for direct human enrollment and verification. This implements the chosen authentication direction; a chat acknowledgement alone must not be fabricated into an authenticated reset. Selecting the mechanism does not authorize clearing the current journal, daily-loss reset, trading or trial startup. Service recovery must preserve the failed evidence, acquire fresh reconciliation and record an authenticated, scoped owner decision. Any daily-loss reset additionally retains the next-NYSE-day, fresh-reconciliation, explicit-human-approval order.

Remaining engineering gates include initial security status and continuous evidence, activity publication/cutoff reconciliation, the owner recovery control, and integrated persistent process/event supervision before any real paper mutation. All executable risk limits and live permission remain unchanged. This work qualifies ETF fixtures and diagnostic routing, not a running trial or an increase to the historical 64% acceptance snapshot.
