# Operational risk decisions — v1.0.1

The [operational decision file](examples/risk-policy-v101/operational-decisions.json) requests owner choices for trusted quote, coherent account snapshot, exchange calendar, clock synchronization, early closures, regular-only trading, extended-hours exclusion, outage/recovery, reservations, kill-switch ownership, exceptions, and human roles. The v1.0 proposed quote and snapshot ages remain **30** and **60 seconds**, respectively, both inactive. Agents may research and monitor 24/7; `BROKER_ORDER_EXECUTION` remains disabled and would be restricted to an approved broker's verified regular session. A `core` tag alone is not proof of an open exchange.

Synthetic fixtures can test timestamp boundaries, holidays/early closures, stale/missing bid and ask, clock skew, broker-capability mismatches, reservation races, and restart recovery without placing an order. Future trusted inputs must carry provider, event and receipt time, symbol/venue/currency, quote basis, status, and calendar version. Historical adjusted bars, news, web pages, and LLM output never become execution quotes or commands. Missing or inconsistent critical evidence always rejects the proposal and blocks new entries.

The [kill-switch decision matrix](examples/risk-policy-v101/operational-decisions.json) distinguishes a temporary fail-closed data halt from a persistent latch. Proposed persistent latches for manual activation, approved loss breaches, confirmed unauthorized execution, and material reconciliation/environment failure await approval. Stale quote or snapshot blocks entries immediately; whether prolonged outage also latches the global switch is still unresolved. Process restart must load persisted switch and reservations before processing; unknown persistence state blocks. Human-authorized cancellation of open orders may be considered after status review, but a cancel request is not a confirmed release, and no automatic liquidation is proposed. An LLM cannot reset the switch.

No exception or override mechanism is implemented. Any future exception must have approved eligible categories, independent roles, explicit scope, expiry, audit and automatic invalidation on policy, portfolio or market changes. No exception may waive missing evidence, environment separation, platform safety, or an unapproved hard-stop rejection.
