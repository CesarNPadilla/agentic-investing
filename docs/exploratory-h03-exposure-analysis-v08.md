# H03 baseline exposure decomposition — diagnostic only

H03 instrumented **unchanged** swing v1.0.0 on 2025 normalized $100,000 capital. Its fills and metrics matched the locked v0.6 baseline exactly: 127 sides, +1.03% return, 12.54× turnover, **29.02% average exposure** and 9 maximum simultaneous positions. Maximum single-name weight observed in the account equity was 15.74%. No strategy behavior, sizing, or exit was changed.

The 249 signal-decision sessions contain 2,241 symbol-session observations across nine symbols. At least one original entry signal appeared on **52 sessions**; 197 had none. There were **64** technically eligible symbol-sessions, at most three symbols eligible on one session. Constraint tags are **overlapping**, not portions of a 100% pie: 1,444 nonheld observations lacked an entry signal, 733 observations had an existing position, and 126 observations coincided with pending-order timing. The runner saw no whole-share unaffordability at $100,000; cash was not the primary observed constraint at that capital. Warmup/missing-data tags were zero in this 2025 decision window. The fixed 10%-of-starting-capital entry target and at-most-three contemporaneous eligible names also constrain deployment; there is no separately configured maximum-position-count limit in this simulator. These are descriptive mechanisms, not causal shares of the 29.02% exposure gap.

The [machine-readable H03 summary](examples/exploratory-retrospective-v08/final/h03-summary.json) retains per-symbol candidate and constraint records. It distinguishes original technical eligibility, existing holdings, pending fills, cash/whole-share estimates, exposure, and concentration. Cash and hypothetical allocation are simulated; no real portfolio was read. **Risk Engine outcomes: `NOT_EXERCISED`.** There were no observed historical Risk Engine approvals or rejections. The simulator created counterfactual fills only, never broker orders.

The 2025 period is research-informed. Current-vintage adjustment, action coverage, benchmark, and survivorship limitations still apply. H03 is diagnostic only and grants no qualifying or paper eligibility.
