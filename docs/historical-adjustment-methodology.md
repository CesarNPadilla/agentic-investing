# Historical adjustment methodology v0.5

The engine reads immutable `RawHistoricalPriceBar` observations and a security-matched ledger. For a split effective at session open, all earlier OHLC prices are divided by the cumulative new/old share ratio and earlier volumes multiplied by it. A forward 10-for-1 split maps 1,208.42 to 120.842 before display rounding; a reverse 1-for-10 split multiplies earlier prices by ten. Held share quantity changes on the effective date, leaving economic value unchanged absent market movement. No adjusted value overwrites a raw bar. Every output records engine version, original raw digest, and applied event IDs.

For a dividend, existing shares become entitled on ex-date; the amount is tracked as a receivable until payment date. Payment moves the receivable to cash exactly once. The price-return series excludes distributions. The cash-dividend total-return series includes shares, cash, and receivables without reinvestment. Reinvested-dividend accounting is an **explicit alternative assumption**: available cash purchases fractional shares at the payment-session close, with no commission in this diagnostic engine. This is not a policy trading permission. The model assumes a position held before ex-date and does not model intraday acquisition, tax withholding, fractional cash-in-lieu, nontrading payment dates, or broker settlement. Those cases require more evidence or a fuller ledger.

The engine returns `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` if event coverage or terms are unverified, a payment session is missing, or supplied sessions are misaligned. Duplicate events, wrong security, unordered/OOS bars, or mixed raw/adjusted input are rejected. Event completeness cannot be inferred from an empty feed response.
