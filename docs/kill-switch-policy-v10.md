# Kill-switch policy — proposal

**DESIGN ONLY.** The existing hypothetical evaluator requires a fresh, explicitly disengaged switch; a missing/stale/active state fails closed. A future persistent switch must block every new order submission and approval-to-submission transition across process restarts and environments. It must not by itself liquidate holdings.

Manual activation is immediate by an authorized human. Proposed automatic activation conditions are a breached approved daily or cumulative sleeve stop, a breached whole-account stop once defined, confirmed unauthorized submission attempt, unreconciled broker/account state, material environment mismatch, or repeated order failures. For missing policy, stale quote, uncertain session, or provider outage, block entries immediately; whether these also latch the global switch versus a scoped data halt is `UNRESOLVED` and must be approved before implementation. No arbitrary repeated-failure count is active. Every trigger must have a stable code, source evidence, timestamp, affected scope, and immutable audit event.

An active switch forbids new submissions; whether to cancel open orders requires a separate authenticated operational decision based on broker status, partial fills, and cancellation risk. Cancellation is not assumed successful until confirmed. No automatic liquidation. Only an authorized human can reset after cause remediation, fresh reconciliation, and documented review; an LLM/agent cannot reset it. A reset does not bypass any other policy check. Restart loads the persisted switch before accepting work; missing persistence state means killed/fail closed. Production and paper switches require environment isolation and independently tested credentials.
