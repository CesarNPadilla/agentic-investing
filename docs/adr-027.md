# ADR-027: separate nonexecutable risk-policy proposal from current authority

Status: design proposal awaiting human approval (v1.0).

Keep current Investment Policy and risk evaluator unchanged. Record proposed $1,000 sleeve and numerical risk limits in documents and explicitly nonexecutable JSON only. Separate experimental daily/cumulative losses from an unresolved whole-account production daily stop. Apply platform-to-human-approval precedence; no lower layer weakens a hard stop. Future v1.1 work needs approved schema, trusted quote/session evidence, atomic reservations, persistent kill switch, independent loss checks, reconciliation, and separate deployment authorization. This ADR creates no trading permission.
