# Daily and cumulative loss stop proposal v1.0

The authorized production/account daily-loss hard stop is **UNSET and fail closed**. No value below is active. Proposed experimental daily stop: lesser of $20 and 2% of positive start-of-day flow-adjusted sleeve equity, in USD, `PROPOSED_REQUIRES_APPROVAL`. Proposed cumulative sleeve stop: lesser of $100 and 10% of initial funded sleeve capital, `PROPOSED_REQUIRES_APPROVAL`. These are independent limits; neither substitutes for a separately approved whole-account/production stop.

The proposed calendar-day reset boundary is **00:00 America/New_York**, including daylight-saving transitions. Use the last reconciled closing equity as the carried boundary reference; overnight price changes count when the first fresh mark arrives, and no new entry is allowed before that mark and reconciliation. Daily P&L equals current marked equity minus boundary equity minus net deposits plus withdrawals, including realized and unrealized movements and fees. A separate cumulative baseline is fixed at owner-approved sleeve creation; cash flows alter equity but not the loss baseline or authorized cap. Require complete transaction, position, and price evidence; do not infer P&L from cash alone. Recompute on each proposal, each broker/account event and fresh mark, and immediately before any future submission. Exact sampling interval and trustworthy event/mark feed remain implementation questions; stale marks block entries.

At or below either proposed negative sleeve limit, block all new entries, persist an active kill switch, freeze unused reservations, and require an authorized human to review open orders and existing positions. A cancellation may be considered through a separate authenticated operational procedure; it must not be assumed safe or automatically sent. No automatic liquidation. The daily boundary alone does not clear the switch: require a fresh reconciled snapshot, a new valid day baseline, and explicit human reset. Cumulative breach remains latched across days and deposits until a separately approved new baseline. Restarts reload persisted breach and switch state before any evaluation. Every calculation, input digest, trigger, attempted action, decision, and reset is audit recorded.

Whole-account daily loss should be independently enforced on flow-adjusted account-wide equity, including holdings outside the sleeve. Its threshold and exact ownership remain `UNRESOLVED`; the experimental $20 proposal must not be written into the current account-wide `max_daily_loss` field. The v1.1 implementation must distinguish `DAILY_SLEEVE_LOSS`, `CUMULATIVE_SLEEVE_LOSS`, and `WHOLE_ACCOUNT_DAILY_LOSS` with separate evidence, limits, and reason codes. Missing or contradictory values block entries.
