# Risk policy approval resolution — v1.0.1

**DECISION PACKAGE ONLY; AWAITING HUMAN APPROVAL.** This document resolves what must be decided, not the decisions themselves. The v1.0 proposal and its seven JSON artifacts remain unchanged. The [machine-readable decision register](examples/risk-policy-v101/decision-register.json) records source SHA-256 digests and initializes every human decision to `PENDING`, with no approver or timestamp.

The ten [numerical decisions](examples/risk-policy-v101/numerical-policy-decisions.json) retain the exact v1.0 proposals: $1,000 attributed experimental allocation; a single-position ceiling of the lesser of $250, 25% initial funded sleeve, 25% current flow-adjusted sleeve equity, and unreserved settled cash; 25% of current sleeve equity for a name; 75% of current positive sleeve equity for aggregate sleeve exposure; $25 minimum whole-share order; daily sleeve loss of the lesser of $20 or 2% positive start-of-day flow-adjusted sleeve equity; cumulative sleeve loss of the lesser of $100 or 10% initial funded sleeve capital; 30-second quote age; 60-second coherent snapshot age; and five-minute approval lifetime subject to quote/state validity. Each has its own ID, measurement, trigger, action, reset, dependency, and blank human response. All are `PROPOSED_REQUIRES_APPROVAL` and inactive. These bounds address capital preservation and stale-input risk; they were not optimized against historical returns.

Capital decisions are independent: **CAP-A** asks whether the future *policy ceiling* may become $1,000; **CAP-B** asks whether $1,000 was actually funded and attributed to a reviewed sleeve baseline; **CAP-C** asks whether a specified environment may use approved funds. A does not imply B or C. The authorized cap is still $160; the existing portfolio is not experimental cash. No real TradeProposal, deposit, account balance change, or brokerage connection is implied.

Use these states precisely: `CURRENT_AUTHORIZED` describes the unchanged baseline; `PROPOSED_REQUIRES_APPROVAL` describes a choice offered to the owner; `UNRESOLVED` means no value or operational owner has been chosen; `DEFERRED_WITH_BLOCKING_GATE` means an operational step cannot open yet; and `APPROVED_BY_HUMAN` requires separate attributable approval evidence. None of the new decisions is approved. Valid human responses are `APPROVE_AS_PROPOSED`, `APPROVE_WITH_REVISION`, `REJECT`, `DEFER`, and `PENDING`. A revision must supply new text and require a fresh consistency review; it does not mutate the v1.0 source.

Roles proposed for approval are policy owner, independent risk-policy reviewer, implementation owner, deployment approver, live-execution approver, and kill-switch reset authority. One person may hold several roles only if explicitly designated; the action log must record the role actually used. No exception path is approved. A possible future exception design could only tighten or selectively relax lower-level *non-safety* sizing within a human-approved upper policy, with bounded scope, expiry, two-role review, immutable audit, and invalidation after material changes. It could never override platform safety, environment isolation, absent evidence, kill switch, or a deterministic hard-stop rejection through an ad hoc approval. Decide whether to permit any such mechanism before implementing one.
