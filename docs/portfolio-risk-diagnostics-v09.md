# Portfolio risk diagnostics v0.9

Technical eligibility, target sizing, whole-share rounding, cash, existing positions, pending orders, and policy acceptance are separate stages. A candidate may be `TECHNICALLY_INELIGIBLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_CAPITAL`, blocked by `EXISTING_POSITION_RESTRICTION` or `PENDING_ORDER_CONSTRAINT`, a `RISK_POLICY_REJECTION`, `MISSING_DATA`, or `NOT_EVALUATED`. Categories may overlap; only a complete snapshot enters a meaningful policy review. Existing holdings outside the buy allowlist are included in exposure and concentration, not liquidated. A buy cannot reduce its own absolute exposure; a concentration reduction can only be a relative portfolio-weight effect, and selling remains unavailable under current buy-only policy.

In the synthetic base, one $100 AAPL share leaves $60 of $160 settled cash and reaches human review. A pending $80 MSFT buy leaves a hypothetical **-$20** after the same proposal; settled cash and sleeve checks reject it. Removing that pending order in a new complete snapshot restores the review outcome. A $500 share marked with the proposed $1,000 scenario still breaches the authorized $160 cap and cash. These are fixture prices, not market observations.

Hard maximum position exposure and concentration are not configured in current policy. The existing no-leverage check rejects projected commitments exceeding reconciled equity, but it is not a substitute for an approved portfolio exposure or concentration maximum. Correlation and simultaneous-position limits are not modeled as hard controls. The v0.8 finding of 98.83% single-name concentration at $1,000 is descriptive, exploratory, and not an acceptable threshold. Historical H03 Risk Engine outcomes remain `NOT_EXERCISED`; no v0.8 results were rerun.
